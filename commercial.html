Why Commercial Clients Choose Us

Built for Commercial Work

C-17 Licensed — We Can Pull Permits

Most glass shops are not licensed to pull permits on commercial properties. As a C-17 licensed glazing contractor, we can legally work on commercial buildings, pull permits, and certify to California building code — which matters for insurance claims, TI buildouts, and lease compliance.

Same-Day Emergency Response

Break-ins and glass failures don't wait for business hours. We offer same-day board-up to secure your property and prioritize permanent glass replacement. We stock common commercial panel sizes for fast turnaround — typically 3–5 business days.

Commercial Account Programs

If you manage multiple properties or storefronts, ask about our commercial account setup — priority scheduling, faster response, and a direct point of contact for ongoing glazing needs across your portfolio.

Documentation for Insurance & Compliance

We provide proper documentation for insurance claims, building department inspections, and commercial lease compliance. All permits are filed under our C-17 license number CSLB #1125850.
