Why It Matters

What C-17 Licensing Means for Your Business

California's C-17 Glazing Contractor license covers the full scope of glass and glazing work on commercial properties — including structural glazing, curtain wall systems, safety glass specification, and permit-required installations. Unlicensed handyman services cannot legally perform this work, and many commercial landlords, property managers, and general contractors require a C-17 license and certificate of insurance before allowing any glazing work on their properties.

American Glass Experts holds CSLB License #1125850 and carries general liability and workers' compensation insurance. We furnish a COI for every commercial job before work begins. For permit-required installations in Los Angeles County, San Bernardino County, and other jurisdictions, we handle the documentation and inspections — you don't need to manage that process separately.

We work with retail chains, restaurant groups, property management companies, general contractors, and independent business owners. Whether you need a single broken storefront panel replaced same-day or a complete glass buildout for a new commercial tenant, our crew handles the full scope under one license.

Commercial Glass Standards

Code Compliance & Safety Glass

California Building Code requires tempered or laminated safety glass in specific locations — near doors and entry points, in shower enclosures, in low-height glazing below 18 inches from the floor, and in areas subject to human impact. On commercial properties, these requirements apply to storefront installations, glass entry doors, office partition panels adjacent to walkways, and conference room walls with glass extending to the floor.

We source glass to the correct specification for each application — tempered to ANSI Z97.1, laminated safety glass for overhead and sloped glazing, Low-E coatings for energy code compliance, and fire-rated glass where required by local jurisdiction. Every installation is performed to code and, where required, submitted for permit and inspection.

Emergency board-up calls are handled without permits when temporary plywood or polycarbonate is used. For permanent replacement glass on commercial storefronts over a certain dollar threshold, we file permits as required — keeping your building compliant and your certificate of occupancy intact.
