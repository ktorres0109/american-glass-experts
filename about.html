Credentials

Licensed, Bonded & Insured

California Contractor License
C-17 Glazing Contractor · CSLB #1125850
The C-17 is California's specialty glazing license — issued by the Contractors State License Board (CSLB). It authorizes us to install, repair, and replace glass in residential and commercial structures. You can verify our license directly at cslb.ca.gov using license number 1125850.
Bonded & Insured
Contractor Bond + General Liability
We carry a California contractor bond and general liability insurance. This protects you if something goes wrong on your property during installation — your home and business are covered.

What Does a C-17 License Mean for You?

California requires a separate specialty license for glazing work — a general contractor's license alone doesn't cover it. A C-17 licensed contractor has passed a state exam specific to glass installation, carries the required bonds and insurance, and is subject to CSLB enforcement if work is substandard.

When you hire an unlicensed glass contractor, you have no CSLB recourse if work fails, and your homeowner's insurance may not cover damage resulting from work by an unlicensed contractor. Always verify the license number before hiring.
