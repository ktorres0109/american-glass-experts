About Our Coverage

Glass Repair Across Southern California

American Glass Experts is based in Reseda in the San Fernando Valley and covers a 90-mile service radius spanning Los Angeles, Ventura, San Bernardino, and Riverside Counties. That puts us within range of the greater Los Angeles metro, the Inland Empire, the Coachella Valley, and the Ventura coast — the densest concentration of residential and commercial glass demand in California.

For residential clients, we handle frameless shower enclosures, window repair and replacement, custom mirrors, and emergency board-up for broken windows and sliding doors. For commercial clients, we install and repair storefront glass, office partition systems, curtain wall panels, and glass entry doors — with same-day emergency response for security-critical openings after break-ins or accidents.

Every job is performed by our licensed crew under California C-17 Glazing Contractor License CSLB #1125850. Unlike handyman services, a C-17 license covers the full scope of glazing work — structural glazing, safety glass specification, aluminum curtain wall, and permit-required installations. This matters on commercial jobs where building departments in Los Angeles and San Bernardino Counties require licensed contractor documentation for glass work above a certain dollar threshold.

We provide free on-site estimates across our entire service area. For cities within Los Angeles County, we typically schedule same-day or next-day estimates. For outlying areas in Riverside and San Bernardino Counties, we consolidate routes to provide estimates within 48 hours. Call (805) 750-6471 to confirm availability for your city.
