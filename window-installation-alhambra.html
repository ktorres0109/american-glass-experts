Window Installation in the Alhambra Valley

The Alhambra Valley housing stock spans Craftsman bungalows from the 1920s through postwar ranch homes and modern commercial buildings along Valley Boulevard and Main Street. That range means window installation here requires real expertise — original wood windows in historic homes require different handling than aluminum retrofit frames in mid-century apartments or the curtain wall panels in downtown office buildings.

We handle all of it. Our C-17 license covers glazing work on residential and commercial properties throughout Los Angeles County. For Alhambra specifically, we do a lot of dual-pane replacements in older single-pane homes — this is the most impactful upgrade for comfort and energy efficiency. If your home still has single-pane aluminum windows, upgrading to dual-pane Low-E cuts summer heat gain dramatically and reduces outside noise. LADWP customers in Alhambra may qualify for rebates — we can confirm at the estimate.

For commercial properties on Valley Blvd, Main Street, and the Alhambra industrial corridor, we install storefront glass, office partition windows, and full curtain wall panels. Call for a commercial quote.

Our Process

1
Free In-Home Estimate

We measure, assess your current windows, and recommend the right glass type and frame. You get a written quote before we start.

2
Glass Ordered to Size

We custom-order your glass units or windows to the exact dimensions of your openings. Standard sizes typically take 2–5 days.

3
Professional Installation

Our licensed glaziers install, flash, seal, and finish the windows. Site is cleaned before we leave.

4
Final Walkthrough

We test operation, check seals, and review the work with you before signing off.
