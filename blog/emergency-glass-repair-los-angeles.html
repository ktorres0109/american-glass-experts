Emergency Glass Repair in LA: What to Do After a Break-In or Accident

By Frank, Lead Glazier  ·  April 2026  ·  C-17 Licensed · CSLB #1125850 · American Glass Experts Inc.

Experiencing a break-in is a deeply unsettling event, and the immediate aftermath can feel overwhelming. Beyond the emotional distress, you're left with a gaping security vulnerability: broken glass. This post will guide Los Angeles homeowners and business owners through the crucial steps to take immediately after a break-in, focusing on how to secure your property with prompt, professional emergency glass repair in Los Angeles.

Prioritize Safety and Document Everything

Before anything else, ensure your safety and the safety of anyone else on the property. If the intruders are still present or you suspect they might be, do not enter; call 911 immediately. Once the police have secured the scene, take detailed photos and videos of all damage, especially the broken glass. Photograph the full extent of the opening, the framing condition, any glass fragments still in place, and any damage to locks or adjacent structure. This documentation is vital for both police reports and your insurance claims — the more thorough your photos before any cleanup begins, the smoother the claims process will be.

Contact Law Enforcement and Your Insurance Provider

After ensuring safety, your next call should be to the Los Angeles Police Department (LAPD) to file a formal report if you haven't already. Obtain a copy of the police report number, as your insurance company will require it for vandalism and break-in claims. Next, notify your homeowners' or business insurance provider about the incident within 24–72 hours — most policies have reporting windows, and missing them can complicate your claim. They can advise you on your coverage for emergency glass repair, board-up costs, and other property damage. Keep all communication with your insurer in writing when possible.

Secure Your Property Immediately with Emergency Glass Repair Los Angeles

A broken window or door is an open invitation for further crime and leaves your property exposed to the elements. This is where professional emergency glass repair in Los Angeles becomes critical. Look for a licensed and insured local glass contractor who offers 24/7 emergency services. They can quickly board up the opening to restore temporary security, take measurements, and schedule the permanent glass replacement, often within the same visit or very soon after.

Understanding the Repair Process and What to Expect

When your emergency glass repair technician arrives, they will first assess the damage and measure the opening. For standard glass types in common sizes, they might have replacement glass on hand. For custom sizes, tempered glass, or specialized insulated glass units common in many newer LA homes and commercial properties, a temporary board-up will be installed on the first visit — the new glass is then fabricated to the exact dimensions and installed within 1–5 business days. Always ask for a written estimate itemizing board-up and glass replacement costs separately before any work begins, as your insurance may cover these differently.

Choosing the Right Glass for Future Security and Energy Efficiency

After the immediate crisis, consider upgrading your glass for enhanced security and energy efficiency. Standard annealed glass — what many older windows and doors use — is easy to break and dangerous when it shatters. Options like tempered safety glass, laminated security glass, or window security film can offer significantly more protection against future break-ins. Laminated glass, in particular, holds together even when cracked, which buys critical time and noise that deters opportunistic theft. Discuss these options with your glazing contractor to find solutions that fit your budget and provide long-term protection, especially in areas of Los Angeles and the Valley that see higher rates of smash-and-grab incidents.
