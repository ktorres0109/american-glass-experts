About This Service

Glass partitions are a growing category as businesses convert warehouse and open-plan space into functional offices. We install full-height frameless glass partitions, aluminum-framed partition systems, and partial-height dividers with glass lites.

All commercial partition work is done under permit when required by the building department. As C-17 licensed glazing contractors, we can pull permits and work on commercial properties.

The same systems work at home: room dividers, glass walls, and shower glass walls. We measure on site and quote after seeing the space.
