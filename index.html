Who We Are

Built on Glass

American Glass Experts Inc. is a licensed glass company based in Reseda, CA — serving the San Fernando Valley and all of Southern California. Our crew brings more than 20 years of combined hands-on glazing experience to every job.

We specialize in glass partition design, storefront glazing, and window systems. We work closely with contractors, architects, and homeowners to make sure every project is finished on time and within budget. Any size job — we show up, do it right, leave the site clean.

We make the glass. We come to you. We install it. You just wait for the finished product.
